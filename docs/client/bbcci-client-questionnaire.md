# Made in Bangladesh Expo Portal — Client Questionnaire

19 October 2026

These questions cover everything we need to design and build the Made in Bangladesh Expo portal: website, registration, exhibitor and association portals, payments, visas and BBCCI's admin panel.

Questions marked ★ shape the design, so please answer those first. The rest can follow later or in a short call with us. Short answers are fine, and "not decided yet" is a useful answer: we will propose a default.

## 1. Materials to send

Please share whatever you already have; tick each item as you send it.

- [ ] Your requirements brief or scope document for the portal, if you have one
- [ ] Logo source files (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF) for BBCCI and for the expo, including the "BB" monogram used on the 2025 backdrop

- [ ] Original high-resolution photos and videos from Expo 2025 and other BBCCI events (camera originals, not website copies)

- [ ] Current terms and conditions, refund or cancellation policy, and privacy policy
- [ ] Board and committee list with titles, photos and short messages
- [ ] Logos of partners, sponsors and supporting organisations, with permission to use them
- [ ] Press coverage links from 2025
- [ ] Testimonials from 2025 exhibitors or buyers, with names and permission
