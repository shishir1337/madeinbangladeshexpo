# Competitor demo video: end-to-end walkthrough (translated)

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w11uhmrBUA8 (Javed IT Solution, Bangla narration).
Original transcript: `youtube_transcript.txt` in the project root. Below is a faithful English translation, organised by section.

## The pitch (problem framing)

"A Bangladeshi exporter wants a stall at the São Paulo expo. What do they have to do today? Emails, PDF forms, photos of bank slips, WhatsApp follow-ups, and a pile of paper on BBCCI's own desk. On the new Made in Bangladesh Expo website and portal, the whole journey sits behind one login: from application to invoice, payment to visa checklist, in three languages."

## 1. Public website

- Homepage **hero slider** with real photos from 2025.
- **Countdown** to 22–25 March 2027, Pro Magno Centro de Eventos, São Paulo.
- **Trade-route globe**: sea route Chattogram → Santos port; air route Dhaka → São Paulo.
- **Nine sectors**, each with its own page.
- **Timeline** of bilateral relations from 1972.
- **One-click Portuguese** version of the whole site for Brazilian buyers; **Bangla** version with Bangla numerals.
- 2025 **gallery**, **programme**, **newsroom**, **searchable FAQ**.
- **City guide**: hotels, metro, embassy, all on a map.

## 2. Brazilian buyer journey (Portuguese)

- Registration in **4 steps**.
- Enter **CNPJ** → company name and address auto-fill. Enter **CEP** → street address auto-fills.
- Every **consent in its own box**, none pre-ticked.
- **Autosave**: if the browser closes mid-way, the form is saved; on return you resume where you left off.
- On submit: instant **QR badge** on screen, by email, and as PDF.
- **Self-service**: fix a name typo yourself; resend the badge.
- **Group registration**: a company bringing 10 people uploads an Excel file; each person gets their own badge.

## 3. Exhibitor journey (Bangladeshi exporter)

- Create account → portal → **5-step application**:
  1. Company info: BIN, TIN, trade licence
  2. Products (and medicines). If cosmetics or food, **ANVISA** questions appear here.
  3. Document upload
  4. Package selection, with price shown instantly in **USD and BDT**
  5. Accept terms: **date, time and terms version are recorded**
- **Application number** generated.
- When BBCCI approves: **proforma invoice** and **participation contract** PDFs in the applicant's language.
- **Payment, two ways**:
  - Upload bank slip → when verified, **receipt** issued
  - Online: card, **bKash**, **Nagad**, **SSLCommerz** (goes live once the BBCCI merchant account is connected)
  - **Instalments 50/50**
- **Exhibitor portal**:
  - Company profile in 3 languages
  - Product list
  - **Task checklist** that advances automatically as tasks complete
  - **Stall name** (fascia)
  - **Staff badges**
  - **Encrypted document vault**
  - Team members
  - **Travel & visa desk**: passport details; site warns if validity is under 6 months; embassy **16-item checklist** with an upload per item

## 4. Association portal

- Separate portal for associations (e.g. **BGMEA**): own **referral link**, **quota**, list of who applied, **Excel export**.

## 5. For BBCCI (admin)

- Change **dates, venue, fees in one place** → the whole site, all 3 languages, emails and PDFs update together.
- **50+ email templates** in 3 languages; **log of every sent email**.
- **Audit trail** of every change.
- **Privacy**: consent records designed around Brazil's **LGPD** and Bangladesh's data-protection law; sensitive documents **encrypted**; **auto-deleted** after retention period.
- Checked on all screen sizes, mobile to large screens.
- SEO: **sitemap**, **structured data**, "São Paulo, Brazil" in every page title.
- When an exhibitor is approved: their own **public directory page**, shareable.

## 6. Admitted as NOT done yet (per the video)

"So far only a small part of the work is done: the public website and the participants' full journey. Still remaining:"

- BBCCI's own **console with a guided mode**, so everything can be run without a developer
- **Finance & visa desk**
- **QR check-in**
- **Campaigns**

> Takeaway: their admin/ops side (console, finance desk, visa desk, on-site check-in, campaigns) is unfinished. This is the area BBCCI staff will use every day, and our biggest opening.
